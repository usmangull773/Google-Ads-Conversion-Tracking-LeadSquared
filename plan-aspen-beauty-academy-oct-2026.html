01

What we found

A quick public review of both pages shows the foundations are in place: GTM is installed, Google Ads is loading, and leads come in through Gravity Forms. The gap is in the middle. The click ID is not being captured at the form, so LeadSquared has no way to tell Google Ads which enrollments came from which click.

The landing page form drops ad attribution before the lead reaches LeadSquared

High impact

The inquiry form on info.aspenlaurel.edu (Gravity Forms, ID 1) collects first name, last name, email and phone. There are no hidden fields for GCLID, GBRAID, WBRAID or UTM values, and no LeadSquared tracking script on the page. Without these, offline conversion uploads have nothing to match against.

Today
Ad click with GCLID>Landing page>Form: name, email, phone only>LeadSquared: source unknown
After setup
Ad click with GCLID>GCLID and UTMs stored for 90 days>Hidden fields filled on submit>LeadSquared fields mapped>Milestones sent to Google Ads

Overlapping tags on the main site risk double counted data

High impact

aspenlaurel.edu loads two GTM containers, a GA4 tag placed directly in the page code, and a Universal Analytics ID that Google retired in 2024. The landing page runs one container and the Google Ads tag through GTM. Before any new conversions are added, these need to be reconciled so each event fires once.

Form and phone conversions need to measure real outcomes

Medium impact

The form submits by AJAX, so there is no thank-you page URL to track. The conversion has to fire on the Gravity Forms confirmation event, not on a button click. Phone links point straight to the school numbers, so a tap on the number is counted, but whether the call connected is not.

Counts the wrong thing

  • Submit button clicks, including failed validation
  • Phone number taps with no call
  • Form views or page loads

Counts what matters

  • Confirmed Gravity Forms submissions only
  • Connected calls over a set duration via forwarding numbers
  • Tours, applications and enrollments from LeadSquared

1

Audit and clean up every tag

  • Review both GTM containers, GA4 and every Google Ads conversion action
  • Consolidate into one container, remove UA and duplicate GA4, add GA4 to the landing page
  • Fire the lead conversion on the Gravity Forms confirmation event only

Why: new conversions built on top of duplicate tags produce numbers nobody can trust.

2

Capture GCLID and UTMs and map them into LeadSquared

  • Confirm auto-tagging, store GCLID, GBRAID, WBRAID and UTMs in a first party cookie
  • Add hidden fields to the form, filled on submit, surviving redirects and page changes
  • Map each value to dedicated LeadSquared lead fields with your programmer

Why: the click ID is the key that links an enrollment back to the ad that produced it.

3

Send CRM milestones back to Google Ads automatically

  • Create four offline conversion actions: Tour Scheduled, Tour Completed, Application Submitted, Enrollment
  • Trigger uploads from LeadSquared stage changes through your existing Zapier account, with exact milestone timestamps
  • One upload per lead per milestone for duplicate prevention, plus a failed upload alert
  • Turn on enhanced conversions for leads using hashed email and phone

Why: Google Ads can only bid toward enrollments if it is told when they happen.

4

Track calls that connect, not just taps

  • Set up Google forwarding numbers on the site and call assets on ads
  • Count calls over a minimum duration as conversions, keep number taps as a secondary signal
  • Document which call outcomes can be tied to LeadSquared records

Why: calls are a large share of school inquiries and today they are invisible to bidding.

5

Test, document and hand over

  • Live test inquiry traced from click to LeadSquared to Google Ads, with screenshots
  • Recommended primary and secondary conversion setup for reporting and bidding
  • Written guide plus a short walkthrough on what admissions staff must record in LeadSquared
  • Defined post-launch troubleshooting period

Why: the system only stays accurate if staff update lead stages the same way every time.
